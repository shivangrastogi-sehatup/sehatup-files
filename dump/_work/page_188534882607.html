Questions people ask before they start

Straight answers about cost, privacy and what happens next.

Ask us on WhatsApp→
Is the Health Score really free?

Yes. The questionnaire is free and needs no sign-up. You only pay if you decide to order a care kit.

Can Health Score 360 help with PCOS or irregular periods?

It helps you spot the signs, like irregular or painful periods, acne, unwanted hair growth and weight changes, and a doctor then goes through them with you. It does not diagnose PCOS on its own; if needed, your doctor will suggest the right tests.

Can it help with low stamina or performance worries?

Yes. The men's check covers stamina, energy, stress and performance, and a doctor reviews your answers privately before suggesting anything. Many of these concerns are common and treatable.

What happens after I finish it?

A doctor from our team calls you, usually within 24 hours, to go through your answers. You can talk in Hindi or English.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. If your doctor suggests a care kit, they tell you what is in it and what it costs before you decide. Most care kits cost between ₹799 and ₹1,699.

How soon will I see results?

Most people notice first changes in 4 to 6 weeks. A full plan usually runs 3 months, adjusted at each monthly check-in. Results vary from person to person.

How do I pay for a care kit?

Confirm your order with just ₹50 and pay the rest when your kit arrives, or pay the full amount online.

Will anyone know what I ordered?

Your kit is packed discreetly. The shipping label has your address, and product names are cut to their first few letters, so the full names are not shown. Only your doctor sees your answers.

Is it safe to combine Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Allopathy?

Your doctors plan your care together, so the parts of your plan are chosen to work with each other. Always tell your doctor about any medicines you already take.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3 to 5 days, depending on where you live.
