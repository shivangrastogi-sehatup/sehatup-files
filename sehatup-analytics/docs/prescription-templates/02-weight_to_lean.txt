--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   The classic overweight PCOD presentation: weight gain, acne, hairfall and
   irregular periods, usually with insulin resistance and sluggish digestion
   in the background.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   WEIGHT REDUCTION through metabolism and insulin support, plus visible
   improvement in skin (acne) and hair. Bloating and digestion are treated
   as first-order problems, which is why the day starts with methi water and
   ends with a deliberately light dinner.

 HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHERS
   * It is the direct mirror image of template 01 (lean_to_weight_gain).
     Where 01 adds ghee, banana, mango and unrestricted dinner, this plan
     restricts exactly those items. Never confuse the two — picking the
     wrong one gives the patient the opposite advice.
   * The ONLY plan whose early-morning drink is lemon water + methi.
   * The ONLY plan that names "green tea" as the first evening option.
   * Has NO specialFocus card — it is one of two plans without one
     (the other is template 06).
   * One of only two plans (with 01) that prints a HYDRATION card.

 STRUCTURE
   Standard layout minus Special Focus: 6 meal cards (time / food / benefit),
   Daily Add-ons, Foods to Avoid, Lifestyle, Hydration, Follow-Up, Note.
   Because specialFocus is absent, the Daily Add-ons card renders full width
   instead of half width.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, meals(6), dailyAddons, avoidFoods, hydration,
   lifestyle, followUp, note
 FIELDS ABSENT
   specialFocus, understandingItems, advancedTips

 FULL TEMPLATE CONTENT (verbatim from source)

 CONDITION
   PCOD with Weight Gain, Acne, Hairfall & Irregular Periods

 SUBTITLE
   Diet planned considering hormonal imbalance, metabolism disturbance &
   digestive issues


 ── MEAL PLAN (6 meals) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

 [1] EARLY MORNING (7:00–8:00 AM)
     Food    : 1 glass lukewarm water + lemon (without sugar) +
               1 tsp soaked methi seeds
     Benefit : Helps improve digestion, reduce bloating & support fat
               metabolism

 [2] BREAKFAST (8:30–9:30 AM)
     Food    : Option 1: 2 besan chilla + curd
               Option 2: Vegetable oats + seeds mix
               Option 3: 2 eggs + 1 multigrain toast
     Benefit : High protein breakfast helps reduce acne, hairfall &
               controls cravings

 [3] MID-MORNING (11:00–12:00 PM)
     Food    : 1 fruit (apple/guava/papaya)
     Benefit : Maintains energy levels & prevents overeating

 [4] LUNCH (1:30–2:30 PM)
     Food    : 2 multigrain roti + 1 bowl sabzi + 1 bowl dal/paneer +
               salad before meal
     Benefit : Balanced meal supports hormonal balance & weight control

 [5] EVENING SNACK (5:00–6:00 PM)
     Food    : Green tea / normal tea (without sugar) +
               roasted chana / makhana
     Benefit : Controls cravings & supports metabolism

 [6] DINNER (7:30–8:30 PM)
     Food    : 1–2 roti + sabzi OR vegetable soup + paneer
     Benefit : Light dinner helps reduce bloating & supports fat loss


 ── DAILY ADD-ONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Food    : Flax seeds – 1 tsp, Pumpkin seeds – 1 tsp, 2 walnuts
     Benefit : Supports hormonal balance, reduces acne & hairfall


 ── FOODS TO AVOID / LIMIT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • Avoid sugar, bakery, fried & packaged food
     • Limit rice, potato, banana, mango
     • Reduce excess tea/coffee


 ── LIFESTYLE INSTRUCTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • 30–40 min daily walk/exercise
     • Proper sleep (7–8 hrs)
     • Avoid late night eating


 ── HYDRATION ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     2.5–3 litres water daily


 ── FOLLOW-UP ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Review after 15 days


 ── NOTE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     This diet focuses on hormonal balance, weight reduction & improvement
     in skin and hair health.
