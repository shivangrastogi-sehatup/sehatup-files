--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   A PCOD patient who is trying to conceive, or who has been told she has
   ovulation / egg-quality problems, alongside irregular cycles.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   Support OVULATION and reproductive health. Every benefit line is written
   around egg quality, hormone stability and blood flow to the reproductive
   system rather than around weight.

 HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHERS
   * Its meal timings are DELIBERATELY VAGUE. Unlike templates 01, 02 and
     others, the meal `time` labels here carry no clock ranges — they read
     "Breakfast (High Protein)", "Mid-Morning", "Lunch", "Dinner (Light &
     Early)". This is the first of the four "short-form" plans (03, 04, 05
     follow the same compressed style).
   * Special Focus targets Reproductive Health (leafy greens, beetroot,
     nuts, seeds) with blood flow as the stated mechanism.
   * It has the LONGEST lifestyle list of the food+benefit plans (4 items),
     and it is the only one whose lifestyle card ends with a statement of
     principle rather than an instruction ("Regular cycle support is
     essential").
   * NO hydration card and NO note card.

 STRUCTURE
   6 meal cards (time / food / benefit), Daily Add-ons, Special Focus,
   Foods to Avoid, Lifestyle, Follow-Up. No hydration, no closing note.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, meals(6), dailyAddons, specialFocus, avoidFoods,
   lifestyle, followUp
 FIELDS ABSENT
   hydration, note, understandingItems, advancedTips

 CONDITION
   PCOD with Irregular Periods & Infertility Concerns

 SUBTITLE
   Diet planned considering hormonal imbalance, ovulation support &
   reproductive health


 ── MEAL PLAN (6 meals) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

 [1] EARLY MORNING (7:00–8:00 AM)
     Food    : Lukewarm water + 1 tsp soaked methi seeds OR 2 walnuts
     Benefit : Supports hormonal balance & ovulation function

 [2] BREAKFAST (HIGH PROTEIN)
     Food    : Besan chilla + curd  /  Eggs + toast  /  Oats + seeds
     Benefit : Improves egg quality & stabilizes hormones

 [3] MID-MORNING
     Food    : 1 fruit (apple/guava/papaya)
     Benefit : Maintains energy & prevents hormonal fluctuations

 [4] LUNCH
     Food    : 2 roti + sabzi + dal/paneer + salad
     Benefit : Balanced nutrition supports reproductive system

 [5] EVENING SNACK
     Food    : Green tea / normal tea (no sugar) + roasted chana/makhana
     Benefit : Controls cravings & supports metabolism

 [6] DINNER (LIGHT & EARLY)
     Food    : 1–2 roti + sabzi OR soup + paneer
     Benefit : Improves digestion & supports hormonal recovery


 ── DAILY ADD-ONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Food    : Flax seeds, pumpkin seeds, walnuts
     Benefit : Supports ovulation, hormone balance & fertility


 ── SPECIAL FOCUS — Reproductive Health ────────────────────────────────────

 ── FOODS TO AVOID / LIMIT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

 ── LIFESTYLE INSTRUCTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES FOR WHOEVER MAINTAINS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * Because `hydration` is missing, the hydration/follow-up row collapses to
   a single full-width Follow-Up card. If you want a water target on this
   plan's PDF you must add a `hydration` string to the object — adding it to
   the lifestyle bullets will render, but in the wrong card.
 * The second avoidFoods bullet is a rationale, not a food, and renders as
   a bullet next to the real restriction.
 * Only the first meal keeps an explicit clock range; the rest are named
   slots. Keep the style consistent if you edit.
 * Changing any text above requires editing functions/index.js and
   redeploying the Cloud Functions — it is not editable from the CRM.
