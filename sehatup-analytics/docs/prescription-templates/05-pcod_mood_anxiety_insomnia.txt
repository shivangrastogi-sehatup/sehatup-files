--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   A PCOD patient whose dominant complaint is psychological rather than
   physical: mood swings, anxiety, overthinking and disturbed sleep. The
   metabolic side of PCOD is assumed to be secondary here.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   Nervous-system and brain support, blood-sugar stability across the day so
   mood does not crash, and restoring a sleep routine. Note the benefit lines
   are written in mood language ("prevents irritability", "prevents anxiety
   spikes in evening", "improves sleep quality") rather than weight language.

 HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHERS
   * The ONLY plan whose Special Focus card is a ROUTINE rather than a food
     group — "Sleep Support Routine". Its `benefit` field is used unusually:
     instead of stating a benefit it carries a second instruction ("Avoid
     phone 30–45 mins before sleep"). On the PDF that renders in the small
     italic benefit line under the warm-milk recommendation.
   * The only plan that permits banana in the mid-morning fruit list besides
     template 01 (the weight-gain plan) — here for its mood/tryptophan value,
     not for calories.
   * The only plan whose avoid-list names LATE NIGHT EATING as a headline
     item, because it is framed as a sleep problem rather than a weight one.
   * Shortest lifestyle list of any template (2 bullets), one of which is
     again a statement of principle.
   * NO hydration card and NO note card.

 STRUCTURE
   Short-form layout, same shape as templates 03 and 04: 6 meal cards
   (time / food / benefit), Daily Add-ons, Special Focus, Foods to Avoid,
   Lifestyle, Follow-Up.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, meals(6), dailyAddons, specialFocus, avoidFoods,
   lifestyle, followUp
 FIELDS ABSENT
   hydration, note, understandingItems, advancedTips

     Food    : 1 fruit (banana/apple/papaya)

     Food    : 2 roti + sabzi + dal/paneer + salad
     Benefit : Balanced meal supports hormones & mental stability

 [5] EVENING SNACK
     Food    : Herbal tea / normal tea (no sugar) + makhana/chana
     Benefit : Prevents anxiety spikes in evening

 [6] DINNER (LIGHT & EARLY)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES FOR WHOEVER MAINTAINS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * The specialFocus.benefit misuse described above is the one place in all
   six templates where a field is used for something other than its purpose.
   If you ever restyle that card, this string will read wrong.
 * The lifestyle bullets pack multiple instructions into one line
   ("30 min walk, deep breathing, proper sleep routine"). Splitting them
   into separate array entries would render better on the PDF.
 * Both this plan and template 06 mention avoiding late nights / screens,
   but only this one puts it in the Special Focus card.
 * Changing any text above requires editing functions/index.js and
   redeploying the Cloud Functions — it is not editable from the CRM.
