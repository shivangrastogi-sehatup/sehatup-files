--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   An underweight / lean-build PCOD patient. The presenting complaints this
   plan is written around are hairfall, general weakness, and heavy menstrual
   bleeding. It assumes the patient is nutritionally depleted rather than
   insulin-resistant and overweight.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   Gradual healthy WEIGHT GAIN + correcting nutritional deficiency +
   restoring hormonal balance. Improving blood levels (iron/haemoglobin)
   after heavy periods is an explicit secondary goal.

 HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHERS
   * This is the ONLY plan that tells the patient to eat MORE. Dinner
     explicitly carries "No restriction on dinner quantity".
   * It is the only plan that recommends ghee (1-2 tsp daily) and
     seasonal high-sugar fruit (banana / chikoo / mango) as a positive.
     Note that template 02 (weight_to_lean) explicitly restricts those
     same fruits — the two plans are deliberate mirror images.
   * Its Special Focus card targets heavy periods and low blood levels
     (beetroot, spinach, black raisins).
   * It is one of only two plans (with 02) that prints a HYDRATION card.

 STRUCTURE
   Standard 5-card layout: 6 meal cards (time / food / benefit),
   Daily Add-ons, Special Focus, Foods to Avoid, Lifestyle,
   Hydration, Follow-Up, Note.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, meals(6), dailyAddons, specialFocus, avoidFoods,
   hydration, lifestyle, followUp, note
 FIELDS ABSENT
   understandingItems, advancedTips

 FULL TEMPLATE CONTENT (verbatim from source)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 CONDITION
   PCOD (Lean Type) with Hairfall, Weakness, Heavy Periods

 SUBTITLE
   Diet planned considering hormonal imbalance, nutritional deficiency &
   low body weight


 ── MEAL PLAN (6 meals) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

 [1] EARLY MORNING (7:00–8:00 AM)
     Food    : 1 glass lukewarm water + 5 soaked almonds + 2 walnuts +
               5–6 soaked black raisins
     Benefit : Helps improve energy levels, supports hair growth & reduces
               weakness caused by heavy periods

 [2] BREAKFAST (8:30–9:30 AM)
     Food    : Option 1: Paneer sandwich (multigrain bread)
               Option 2: 2 eggs + 1 toast
               Option 3: Vegetable poha with peanuts
     Benefit : High protein + healthy fats help in weight gain, reduce
               hairfall & support hormonal balance

 [3] MID-MORNING (11:00–12:00 PM)
     Food    : 1 banana / chikoo / mango (seasonal)
     Benefit : Supports healthy weight gain & maintains energy throughout
               the day

 [4] LUNCH (1:30–2:30 PM)
     Food    : 2 multigrain roti + 1 bowl sabzi + 1 bowl dal/paneer +
               1 bowl curd  |  Small portion salad before meal
     Benefit : Balanced nutrition improves digestion, supports hormones &
               reduces weakness

 [5] EVENING SNACK (5:00–6:00 PM)
     Food    : Tea (light) + peanut chaat / roasted chana
               OR coconut water + handful makhana
     Benefit : Maintains energy levels & reduces muscle fatigue

 [6] DINNER (7:30–8:30 PM)
     Food    : 2 roti + sabzi + paneer/dal
     Benefit : No restriction on dinner quantity – focus on nourishment
               & recovery


 ── DAILY ADD-ONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Food    : 1 tsp seeds mix (flax + pumpkin) + 1–2 tsp ghee daily
     Benefit : Improves hormonal balance, hair health & supports weight gain


 ── SPECIAL FOCUS — Heavy Periods & Weakness ───────────────────────────────
     Food    : Include beetroot, spinach, black raisins regularly
     Benefit : Helps improve blood levels & reduces fatigue


 ── FOODS TO AVOID / LIMIT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • Avoid junk, packaged food, excess sugar
     • Limit tea/coffee (max 1–2 cups)
     • Prevents further hormonal imbalance


 ── LIFESTYLE INSTRUCTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • 30 minutes light exercise or walk
     • Proper sleep (7–8 hours)
     • Avoid stress & late night eating


 ── HYDRATION ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     2.5–3 litres water daily


 ── FOLLOW-UP ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Review after 15 days for weight and symptom improvement


 ── NOTE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     This diet is specifically designed for lean PCOD patients with weakness
     and heavy menstrual flow. Focus is on improving nutritional status,
     hormonal balance & gradual healthy weight gain.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES FOR WHOEVER MAINTAINS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * The third avoidFoods bullet ("Prevents further hormonal imbalance") is a
   rationale, not a food. It renders as a bullet in the "Foods to Avoid"
   card alongside the two real restrictions.
 * The Follow-Up string here is longer and more specific than the plain
   "Review after 15 days" used by templates 02-05.

   redeploying the Cloud Functions — it is not editable from the CRM.
