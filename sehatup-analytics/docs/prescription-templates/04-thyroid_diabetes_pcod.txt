--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   The most co-morbid of the six: PCOD occurring together with a thyroid
   imbalance AND a sugar tendency (pre-diabetic / diabetic), plus leucorrhea
   (white discharge) and weakness.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   Blood-sugar control and thyroid support at the same time, without letting
   the patient get weaker. Methi seeds open the day specifically for glucose
   control, and every snack is chosen to be low-sugar but anti-fatigue.

 HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE OTHERS
   * IT IS THE DEFAULT TEMPLATE OF THE TEST ENDPOINT. If someone hits
     /testDietPrescriptionPDF with no ?template= parameter, this plan is
     what renders. Useful to know when a test PDF "looks wrong" — it may
     simply be this plan rather than the one you expected.
   * The only plan that addresses leucorrhea. Its Special Focus card
     recommends daytime curd, coconut water and green vegetables.
   * NOTE THE CONTRADICTION with template 06: general_pcod_pcos says
     "1 bowl curd (skip if white discharge)", whereas this plan — the
     leucorrhea plan — actively prescribes curd during the day. If a
     dietician ever revises these, this is the inconsistency to resolve.
   * Adds black raisins to the daily seed mix (only this plan and template
     01 use black raisins).
   * NO hydration card and NO note card.

 STRUCTURE
   Short-form layout, same shape as templates 03 and 05: 6 meal cards
   (time / food / benefit), Daily Add-ons, Special Focus, Foods to Avoid,
   Lifestyle, Follow-Up.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, meals(6), dailyAddons, specialFocus, avoidFoods,
   lifestyle, followUp
 FIELDS ABSENT
   hydration, note, understandingItems, advancedTips

 CONDITION
   PCOD with Thyroid Imbalance, Sugar Tendency, Leucorrhea & Weakness

 SUBTITLE
   Diet planned considering hormonal imbalance, metabolism disturbance &
   nutritional deficiency


 ── MEAL PLAN (6 meals) ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

 [1] EARLY MORNING (7:00–8:00 AM)
     Food    : Lukewarm water + 1 tsp soaked methi seeds
     Benefit : Helps control sugar levels & supports hormonal balance

 [2] BREAKFAST (HIGH PROTEIN)
     Food    : Besan chilla + curd  /  Eggs + toast  /
               Vegetable oats + seeds
     Benefit : Supports thyroid, reduces hairfall & controls cravings

 [3] MID-MORNING
     Food    : 1 fruit (apple/guava/papaya)
     Benefit : Maintains steady energy levels

 [4] LUNCH
     Food    : 2 roti + sabzi + dal/paneer + salad
     Benefit : Improves digestion & supports hormones

 [5] EVENING SNACK
     Food    : Tea (no sugar) + roasted chana/makhana
     Benefit : Prevents weakness & cravings

 [6] DINNER
     Food    : 1–2 roti + sabzi OR soup + paneer
     Benefit : Light dinner improves metabolism & reduces bloating


 ── DAILY ADD-ONS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Food    : Flax seeds, pumpkin seeds, walnuts, black raisins
     Benefit : Helps in hairfall, weakness & hormonal balance


 ── SPECIAL FOCUS — Leucorrhea & Overall Health ────────────────────────────
     Food    : Curd (daytime), coconut water, green vegetables
     Benefit : Supports leucorrhea & overall health


 ── FOODS TO AVOID / LIMIT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • Sugar, junk food, packaged food
     • Limit tea/coffee


 ── LIFESTYLE INSTRUCTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • Daily walk
     • Proper sleep
     • Stress management


 ── FOLLOW-UP ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Review after 15 days

 NOTES FOR WHOEVER MAINTAINS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * This plan has the terse-est lifestyle list of all six (three two-word
   bullets). Considering it is the most co-morbid patient profile, it is
   arguably the one that most deserves detail — worth flagging to the
   dietician if these are ever revised.
 * A diabetic-tendency patient is still being given "1 fruit" and roti
   without any portion guidance. Sugar control here rests entirely on the
   methi water and on avoiding added sugar.
 * The Special Focus benefit line ("Supports leucorrhea & overall health")
   reads oddly — it supports RECOVERY from leucorrhea, not the leucorrhea.
   Verbatim from source; left unchanged here on purpose.
 * Changing any text above requires editing functions/index.js and
   redeploying the Cloud Functions — it is not editable from the CRM.
