--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 WHO IT IS FOR
   The catch-all. Use it when the patient has PCOD with irregular periods but
   does not clearly fall into lean, overweight, fertility-seeking, thyroid/
   sugar or anxiety-dominant. This is the safe default of the six.

 CLINICAL GOAL
   Regulate hormones, improve ovulation and stabilise insulin. Framed as an
   EDUCATIONAL PROTOCOL to be followed for 6–8 weeks rather than a two-week
   corrective diet.

 *** THIS TEMPLATE IS STRUCTURALLY DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER FIVE ***
   Read this before editing it. Templates 01–05 share one shape; this one
   uses a different half of the same schema.

   1. MEALS USE `items[]`, NOT `food` + `benefit`.
      Every other template gives each meal one food string plus an italic
      benefit line. Here each meal is a BULLETED LIST of 3–4 sub-points, and
      there is no per-meal benefit line at all. The Handlebars template
      supports both shapes ({{#if this.food}} vs {{#if this.items}}), so
      both render correctly — but they look noticeably different on the PDF.

   2. IT IS THE ONLY TEMPLATE WITH `understandingItems`.
      This produces the "UNDERSTANDING YOUR CONDITION" card at the top,
      which none of the other five print.

   3. IT IS THE ONLY TEMPLATE WITH `advancedTips`.
      This produces the purple NUMBERED "ADVANCED DIETICIAN TIPS" card,
      including the seed-cycling protocol — advice that appears nowhere
      else in the system.

   4. IT DROPS FOUR CARDS THE OTHERS HAVE.
      No dailyAddons, no specialFocus, no avoidFoods, no hydration,
      no followUp.

   5. LAYOUT CONSEQUENCE (cosmetic bug worth knowing):
      The PDF's "Foods to Avoid | Lifestyle" row is a fixed two-column grid.
      With avoidFoods missing, the Lifestyle card renders in the LEFT column
      and the right half of that row is blank. Likewise the
      hydration/follow-up row renders as an empty container because both
      fields are absent. This is why the general plan's page 2 looks
      emptier than the other five even though it carries more advice.

   6. Its meal slots have EVOCATIVE NAMES rather than clock times —
      "Morning Hormone Reset Routine", "Evening Cortisol Control Snack".
      No other template does this.

   7. It is the only plan that does NOT say "Review after 15 days".
      Its note says follow consistently for 6–8 weeks instead.

 FIELDS PRESENT
   condition, subtitle, understandingItems, meals(6, items-style),
   advancedTips, lifestyle, note
 FIELDS ABSENT
   dailyAddons, specialFocus, avoidFoods, hydration, followUp

 CONDITION
   PCOD with Irregular Periods (General Type)

 SUBTITLE
   Plan focuses on regulating hormones, improving ovulation and stabilizing
   insulin levels


 ── UNDERSTANDING YOUR CONDITION ───────────────────────────────────────────
     • Irregular periods are mainly due to hormonal imbalance.
     • This plan focuses on regulating hormones, improving ovulation and
       stabilizing insulin levels.


 ── MEAL PLAN (6 slots, bulleted style) ────────────────────────────────────

 [1] MORNING HORMONE RESET ROUTINE
     • Warm water with soaked methi seeds (4–5 seeds, 4 times/week)
     • 2 soaked walnuts + 5 almonds
     • 10–15 min sunlight exposure for Vitamin D
     • Light stretching or breathing exercises

 [2] BREAKFAST (HIGH PROTEIN – MUST)
     • Paneer bhurji / Eggs / Moong dal chilla
     • Add 1 tsp flaxseed powder (estrogen balance)
     • Avoid bread, biscuits, sugary cereals
     • Tea/coffee without excess sugar

 [3] MID-MORNING HORMONE SNACK
     • Low GI fruit: Apple / Guava / Papaya
     • 1 tsp pumpkin seeds (zinc for hormone support)
     • Avoid fruit juices

 [4] LUNCH (BALANCED PLATE)
     • 1–2 roti (multigrain) + sabzi + dal
     • Include leafy greens (spinach, methi)
     • 1 bowl curd (skip if white discharge)
     • Avoid fried/oily food

 [5] EVENING CORTISOL CONTROL SNACK
     • Herbal tea (cinnamon + tulsi)
     • Roasted chana / makhana
     • Avoid biscuits, namkeen

 [6] DINNER (LIGHT & EARLY)
     • Dal + sabzi + 1 roti
     • 1 tsp sesame seeds (cycle regulation)
     • Finish dinner before 8:30 PM
     • Avoid heavy carbs at night


 ── ADVANCED DIETICIAN TIPS (numbered on the PDF) ──────────────────────────
     1. Seed Cycling: flax + pumpkin (first half cycle),
        sesame + sunflower (second half)
     2. Protein in every meal improves ovulation
     3. Avoid cold foods during periods
     4. Sleep before 11 PM (melatonin-hormone link)
     5. Daily 20–30 min walk


 ── LIFESTYLE INSTRUCTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
     • Manage stress (yoga, breathing, journaling)
     • Avoid late nights
     • Stay hydrated (2–3 litres water)
     • Consistency is key for results


 ── NOTE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     This is a general plan. Follow consistently for 6–8 weeks for visible
     results.


 ── DAILY ADD-ONS / SPECIAL FOCUS / FOODS TO AVOID / HYDRATION / FOLLOW-UP ─

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES FOR WHOEVER MAINTAINS THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * Hydration guidance exists here, but as a LIFESTYLE bullet ("Stay hydrated
   2–3 litres water") rather than as a `hydration` field, so it does not get
   the water-drop card. Templates 01 and 02 do it the other way round. If
   you want visual consistency, promote it to a `hydration` field.
 * The curd caveat "skip if white discharge" appears ONLY here. Template 04
   — the actual leucorrhea template — recommends daytime curd instead. Worth
   resolving with the dietician.
 * Seed cycling is unique to this plan. If it is genuinely good advice for
   all PCOD patients, it belongs in the other five too.
 * In functions/index.js this object is declared FIFTH in DIET_PLANS, before
   pcod_mood_anxiety_insomnia, even though the CRM dropdown lists it SIXTH
   (last). Lookup is by key so this is harmless — just don't rely on
   positional order anywhere.
 * Changing any text above requires editing functions/index.js and
   redeploying the Cloud Functions — it is not editable from the CRM.
