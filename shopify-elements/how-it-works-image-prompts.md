# How sehatUP Works - step artwork briefs

Image-generation prompts for the four step illustrations, plus the house-style
block that has to travel with every one of them.

Paste **Block A** into ChatGPT first. Then paste **one** variation block per
image. Do not paste several at once - you get muddled hybrids instead of clean
options.

Step 1 is below. Pick a winner, then we do step 2, then 3, then 4. Doing them
one at a time is what keeps the set consistent: each later step is briefed
against the style of the image you actually chose, not against a guess.

---

## 0. Where this artwork actually lives

Four steps sit in one row on desktop, stacked on phones. Everything below is
dictated by that slot:

| Thing | Value | Why it matters to the artwork |
|---|---|---|
| Artwork size | About **200px** wide on desktop, less on a phone | Design it to be judged at 200px, not 1024 |
| Shape | Sits on a soft blush disc | Art must read as one silhouette against `#fdf2f4` |
| Top-left corner | The crimson **01** badge overlaps it | Keep that corner empty or the number covers your art |
| Right edge | A dotted arrow points to the next step | Nothing important within the right 8% |
| Background | The page is white | No baked background, no shadow. The page supplies both |
| Set of four | Steps 1-4 appear side by side | Same eye level, same weight, same colour ratio across all four |

The four steps, so every image knows what it is part of:

1. **Know Your Health Score** - a quick assessment of lifestyle, symptoms and goals
2. **Multi-System Doctor Review** - doctors read the result across several areas
3. **Get a Personalized Care Kit** - a plan and products arrive
4. **Continuous Monitoring** - check-ins and adjustments over time

## Block A - house style (paste this before every variation)

```
You are illustrating one step in a four-step "How it works" strip for SehatUP,
an Indian digital health brand. People come to it for private health concerns:
stamina, hormones, weight, sleep, sexual wellness. They are Indian adults,
25-55, on a phone, deciding whether to trust this. The tone is a calm consulting
room. Not a hospital, not a fitness app, not a tech startup.

BRAND PALETTE - use only these:
  Crimson (primary)   #ee204a
  Deep navy (ink)     #14183c
  Blush (the disc)    #fdf2f4
  Blush line          #f7d9df
  Warm paper          #faf7f6
  Pure white          #ffffff
  Leaf green (one accent, sparingly) #2f6b4f

COLOUR RATIO - keep to this or the four steps will not look like a set:
  Roughly 70% paper and blush, 20% navy, 10% crimson. Crimson marks the one
  thing the customer does or gets. Never use crimson for a whole object.

OUTPUT SPEC:
  - Square canvas, 1024 x 1024, designed to be judged at 200 x 200 pixels.
  - Flat 2D vector illustration. Clean geometric shapes, confident edges,
    one consistent line weight where lines are used.
  - No 3D rendering, no photorealism, no gradient meshes, no bevels, no glow,
    no drop shadow, no outer stroke, no texture, no grain, no sparkles.
  - Plain white background, edge to edge. The page adds the blush disc.
  - Keep all artwork inside a centred circle covering 76% of the canvas.
  - Leave the top-left eighth of that circle empty. A number badge sits there.
  - Nothing important in the right 8% of the canvas. An arrow points out of it.

THE 200-PIXEL RULE - this overrides every aesthetic instinct:
  - Minimum thickness of any line or limb: 4% of the circle diameter.
  - Minimum gap between any two shapes: 3% of the circle diameter.
  - Maximum 6 to 8 distinct shapes in the whole illustration. Count them.
  - Faces are 3 to 5 shapes: head, hair, two eye dots, one mouth line. No
    pupils, eyebrows, nostrils, individual fingers, hair strands or fabric folds.
  - If a detail would be smaller than a grain of rice on a phone, delete it.

HARD BANS:
  - No text, letters, numbers, wordmarks, UI labels or fake interface copy.
    Any screen or card shown must be blank shapes only.
  - No red cross or white-cross-on-red. It is the Red Cross emblem and it is
    legally protected. Use a stethoscope, a leaf or a pulse line.
  - No caduceus. If a medical staff is needed, use the single-snake Rod of
    Asclepius.
  - No syringes, needles, IV drips, hospital beds, ECG monitors, or anything
    that reads as clinical or frightening.
  - No stock-photo smiles, thumbs up, checkmark confetti, laurel wreaths,
    shields, or glowing auras.
  - No Western white lab coat as the only signifier of a doctor. A stethoscope
    does that job.
  - No generic app mockup: no rounded-rectangle phone frame with fake charts
    unless the variation explicitly asks for a device.

Confirm you have understood, then wait for the variation brief.
```

## Step 1 - "Know Your Health Score"

The card reads: *Take a quick assessment that looks at your lifestyle, symptoms
and health goals.*

What this image has to say, in order of importance:
1. It is quick and easy. Nothing here is an exam.
2. It is about **you**, not about a device.
3. It produces a single clear result, which is what earns the next step.

The three variations attack it from three different angles. They are not three
versions of one idea, so judge them on which story you want to tell, not on
which is prettiest.

---

### 1A. The dial - "one number, in a minute"

The result itself, with no device around it. This is the one that avoids the
phone-mockup look entirely and scales down best.

```
VARIATION 1A - THE SCORE DIAL

A single open arc, like a speedometer sweep, drawn as one thick band with
rounded ends. It occupies the middle of the circle. A small filled marker sits
about two-thirds along the arc, showing a good but improvable score.

Composition: the arc's opening faces down. Below the arc's centre, three short
horizontal bars of decreasing width suggest the answers that produced it -
abstract, no text. The whole thing reads as one object, centred, calm.

Colour: the arc band is blush line #f7d9df for its full length, and the
travelled portion up to the marker is crimson #ee204a. The marker is crimson
with a white centre. The three bars below are deep navy #14183c at three
different lengths. Background plain white.

Shape budget: arc, crimson arc portion, marker, three bars. Six shapes.

No needle, no gauge ticks, no percentage, no numerals anywhere, no face, no
device frame, no hand.
```

---

### 1B. The person answering - "it is a conversation, not a form"

Puts a human in the first step. Warmer, and sets up the doctor in step 2 as the
same visual family.

```
VARIATION 1B - SOMEONE ANSWERING

One Indian adult, head and shoulders, turned three-quarters toward the viewer,
mid-answer. Relaxed shoulders. Not a patient, not a model: an ordinary person
thinking about a question.

Composition: the figure sits slightly right of centre so the top-left stays
empty. To their left, three small rounded chips float at different heights,
like answers being picked. The chips are blank shapes, no text inside.

Colour: figure in warm paper #faf7f6 with deep navy #14183c hair and features.
Two of the chips are blush #fdf2f4 with a blush line #f7d9df edge; the third,
the one nearest the figure, is solid crimson #ee204a. That single chip is the
only crimson in the image.

Face budget: head shape, hair shape, two eye dots, one calm mouth line.

The person's gender should be readable as either. No jewellery, no bindi, no
logo, no clothing pattern, no hands, no phone.
```

---

### 1C. The body map - "it looks at all of you"

Sells the "lifestyle, symptoms and goals" breadth, and plants the idea that step
2 reviews several systems. The most distinctive of the three, and the one that
makes the four-step story hold together.

```
VARIATION 1C - THE BODY MAP

A simple standing human figure seen from the front, drawn as one flat silhouette
with no internal detail, centred in the circle.

Composition: four small dots sit on the figure at head, chest, stomach and legs,
each connected by a short straight line to a small blank rounded card floating
to the right of the body. The cards are stacked at even spacing. It reads as a
body being checked in four places at once.

Colour: the silhouette is deep navy #14183c. Three of the dots and their cards
are blush #fdf2f4 with a blush line #f7d9df edge. One dot and its card, the
chest one, is crimson #ee204a. Background plain white.

Shape budget: silhouette, four dots, four connector lines, four cards.

The figure has no face, no hair detail, no gender markers and no clothing. Keep
the connector lines horizontal and the cards clear of the right 8% of the canvas.
```

## Judging the three

Put all three on the page at 200px before deciding. Ask:

- Does it still read at arm's length on a phone?
- Does the crimson land on the thing the customer gets, not on decoration?
- Can steps 2, 3 and 4 be drawn in the same language? 1B and 1C both scale to a
  set easily. 1A is the cleanest on its own but hardest to extend, because a
  dial has no sequel.

Tell me which one wins and I will brief step 2 against it.
