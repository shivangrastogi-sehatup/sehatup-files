SEPARATING A SCENE
1. Person: remove.bg (or Photoshop "Remove background") on the full scene, then crop tight to the person and save as PNG.
2. Background: in ChatGPT/Photoshop, erase the person (Generative fill / "remove the person and continue the background naturally, keep everything else the same"), save as WebP or JPG.
3. Or skip separating: upload the whole scene as the Background image only, with no Foreground image. Easier, but on phones the person gets cropped, which is why separating is better.
