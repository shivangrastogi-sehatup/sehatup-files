FOUR PROMPTS FOR THE "CONDITIONS WE SOLVE" SECTION
Each prompt is self-contained. Use one at a time. Nothing is attached, so the
generator has no existing layout to copy.


=====================================================================
SHARED BRIEF (already written into each prompt below, repeated here for you)
=====================================================================
Brand: sehatUP, an Indian integrated health clinic that combines Ayurveda,
Homeopathy and Modern Medicine. Audience: Indian adults, 25 to 50, browsing
on a phone or laptop, who are not sure whether their problem is "a real
condition" or just life. The job of this section: in one glance, the reader
must find their own problem in the list and believe it is treatable here.

Colour: white and off-white base, near-black #1d1d1d for headings, grey
#55555f for body text, pale pink #fdf4f6 as a quiet surface, crimson #ee204a
as a spot accent used two or three times in the whole section at most.
Never a red section. No gradients, no glows, no drop shadows under every box.

Type: one geometric sans (Poppins-like). Bold headings, regular body,
sentence case. No all-caps labels, no tracked-out eyebrow text.

Text to set exactly:
  Heading: Conditions we Solve
  Subtitle: We don't guess. We read your symptoms, your lifestyle and your
  body signals, then build a doctor-guided plan from Homeopathy, Ayurveda
  and Modern Medicine.
  Sexual Wellness - Performance, Desire, Stamina
  Hormonal Imbalance - PCOS, Irregular Cycles, Mood
  Weight Gain and Metabolism - Belly Fat, Slow Metabolism, Cravings
  Stress and Anxiety - Restlessness, Overthinking, Tension
  Sleep Issues - Trouble Sleeping, Light Sleep, Restlessness
  Digestion and Bloating - Gas, Acidity, Constipation
  Low Energy and Fatigue - Tiredness, Weakness, Low Drive
  Immunity - Frequent Cold, Low Resistance
  Button: Take a 2-minute Health Score Quiz
Spell "sehatUP" exactly: one word, lowercase s, capital UP.
No other words anywhere. No browser frames, no cursors, no watermarks.

PROMPT A - THE BODY MAP
=====================================================================
Design one desktop web section as a single flat image, 2400 by 1500 pixels,
on a white background.

The section is built around one calm, simplified human figure drawn in line
art, standing centred, filling most of the height. The figure is neutral and
non-clinical: soft continuous lines in near-black #1d1d1d on a pale pink
#fdf4f6 oval that sits behind it. Not a muscle chart, not an anatomy
diagram, no organs on show, no skeleton.

Eight short labels sit around the figure, four on the left and four on the
right, each joined to the part of the body it belongs to by a thin hairline
with a small dot where it touches: sexual wellness low, hormonal imbalance
and immunity mid-torso, weight gain and metabolism at the waist, digestion
at the stomach, stress and anxiety and sleep at the head, low energy across
the chest. Each label is a bold condition name in #1d1d1d with its three
symptoms underneath in grey #55555f at about half the size.

Heading and subtitle sit above the figure, centred, with the heading large.
The quiz button sits below the figure, centred: a crimson #ee204a pill with
white bold text. Crimson appears only on that button and on the small
connector dots. Everything else is ink, grey and pale pink.

Generous white space. Nothing is boxed. This design has no cards at all.

PROMPT B - THE QUIET INDEX
=====================================================================
Design one desktop web section as a single flat image, 2400 by 1400 pixels,
on an off-white background.

The eight conditions are set as a strict two-column index, four rows on the
left and four on the right, separated by a single vertical hairline down the
middle. Each row is one line of hairline rule, then the condition name in
bold #1d1d1d at a large, confident size, with its three symptoms on the same
line pushed to the right edge of its column in grey #55555f. Rows are tall
and airy, like a well-set contents page in a book.

To the left of each condition name sits one small icon, 32px, drawn in a
single consistent line weight in #1d1d1d, never filled, never in a circle:
a crescent for sleep, a leaf for digestion, a spark for energy, a shield for
immunity, a wave for stress, a scale for weight, a cycle arrow for hormones,
a heart for sexual wellness. All eight icons must look like one family drawn
by one hand.

Heading and subtitle sit top left, left aligned, not centred, with the
subtitle in a column no wider than 55 characters. The quiz button sits at
the bottom right of the index, aligned to the right column: a crimson
#ee204a pill with white bold text. That button is the only crimson in the
image, apart from one thin crimson rule under the heading.

Editorial and restrained. No cards, no rounded boxes, no shadows.

PROMPT C - EIGHT ILLUSTRATED TILES
=====================================================================
Design one desktop web section as a single flat image, 2400 by 1600 pixels,
on a white background.

Eight tiles in a four by two grid, all exactly the same size, generous gaps
between them, corners rounded to about 20px. Each tile has a pale pink
#fdf4f6 surface, no border and no shadow. Inside each tile, in this order
top to bottom: a small illustration, the condition name in bold #1d1d1d, and
its three symptoms in grey #55555f.

The illustrations are the point of this design. Each is a small three
dimensional style object on a soft circular pink base, warm and friendly,
the kind of clay-render object used in modern health apps: a moon and pillow
for sleep, a bowl and leaf for digestion, a battery half filled for low
energy, a shield with a tick for immunity, a knotted string coming loose for
stress, a tape measure around a soft round shape for weight, a cycle of two
arrows for hormones, two overlapping hearts for sexual wellness. One
consistent palette across all eight: cream, warm sand, soft navy #14183c and
one crimson #ee204a detail in each. No photographs, no faces, no stock
illustration people.

Heading and subtitle centred above the grid. Below the grid, a single wide
pale pink bar holding the line "Not sure which one is yours?" on the left
and the crimson quiz button on the right.

PROMPT D - SYMPTOM TO PLAN
=====================================================================
Design one desktop web section as a single flat image, 2400 by 1500 pixels,
on a white background.

The section reads left to right as one sentence. On the left, a tall pale
pink #fdf4f6 panel titled with the heading and subtitle. In the middle, the
eight conditions stacked as eight slim rows, each row a name in bold
#1d1d1d with its symptoms beneath in grey #55555f, and a small line icon at
the left of each. On the right, one single card, taller than the rows and
clearly the destination: a white card with a thin crimson #ee204a border
holding a simple drawing of three prescription slips fanned out, labelled
Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Allopathy in small text.

Thin grey hairlines run from every one of the eight rows to the right and
converge into the left edge of that one card, so the eye sees eight problems
arriving at one plan. The convergence is the memorable moment of the design,
so keep everything else quiet.

The quiz button sits underneath the card on the right: a crimson pill with
white bold text. Crimson appears only on the card border, the convergence
point and the button.

=====================================================================
NOTES
=====================================================================
A and D explain something; B and C are faster to read. If the goal is
"one glance, points drilled in", C is the safest and B is the most grown up.
A is the one people remember, and it is also the one an image model is most
likely to get wrong, so try it first and judge the figure carefully.
