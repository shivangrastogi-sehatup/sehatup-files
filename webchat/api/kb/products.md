# What each product is actually for

The LIVE CATALOG block carries names, prices and stock. This file carries the thing prices
cannot tell you: **who each product is for, and which one to reach for**. When the two
disagree about a price, the live catalog wins. When they disagree about purpose, this file
wins - the store descriptions are marketing copy and are sometimes vague.

Written from the SehatUP team's own briefing. Keep it updated when the range changes.

male physical health and energy, supports regaining strength and getting over tiredness,

continued use. If someone describes finishing too quickly, low confidence in bed, or
performance anxiety, this is the product to lead with.

to Vaji Bati for every men's-performance message.

**Confidence & Performance Booster Kit** - the kit for BOTH premature ejaculation AND
erectile difficulty together. Homeopathic plus ayurvedic. This is the combo to pitch when
someone describes both problems, or describes PE and ED without separating them. It is
over the counter.

**Boldup 100 mg** - sildenafil citrate. PRESCRIPTION ONLY. Never price it, never link it,
never bring it up yourself.
